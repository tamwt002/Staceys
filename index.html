Our story

It started with one espresso machine, a milk crate of records and way too many plants.

We wanted the kind of place we'd actually hang out in. Somewhere with good coffee, chairs rescued from hard rubbish, a record always spinning and nobody rushing you out the door. So we built it, mostly by hand and slightly crooked.

These days the plants have taken over, the records have multiplied and our regulars have become mates. Come in for a long black, leave with a new favourite album.
