Our story

It started with one espresso machine, a milk crate of records and way too many plants.

We wanted the kind of place we'd actually hang out in. Then we found it: a tired old two storey house with a wild back garden and a lemon tree. We put the coffee machine out in the garden, filled the house with records and hard rubbish couches, and fixed everything slightly crooked.

These days the garden is where half the street has its morning coffee. And on Friday and Saturday nights the whole house turns into the kind of party you used to have in share houses, just with better speakers.

Fri + Sat in the House

A few house rules

It's a house party, not a free for all. Five things, that's it.

HELLO my name is

  1. Wear your name badge

    You'll get one at the door. Keep it on the whole time you're in the House, so nobody's a stranger for long.

  2. Swap your little something

    Everyone gets an ornament or trinket on arrival. Your job is to swap it with someone else before the night's out.

  3. Themed night? Go all in

    We expect full participation. Costumes, colours, whatever the theme asks for. Half the fun is seeing what everyone turns up in.

  4. BYO, but make it local

    Bring your own drinks if they're locally made, brewed or produced, or from a locally owned business. Or grab something from our bar.

  5. Don't be a dickhead

    Pretty simple, really.
